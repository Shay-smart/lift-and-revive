What your first visit looks like

About 75 minutes in total. Come without makeup if you can.

Consult

Fifteen minutes on your skin history, medications and what you want to change. We look at your skin under magnification before recommending anything.

Treat

A treatment matched to what we found, not what you booked. If a gentler option is the right call, we will switch and adjust the price down.

Written aftercare before you leave and a message from your aesthetician five days later. Rebooking is optional and never automatic.

Common questions

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?

For facials, no. Your first appointment includes a skin analysis before we begin. Injectable treatments do require a separate consultation with our nurse injector first.

How much downtime should I plan for?

Most facials, LED and body treatments have none at all. Microneedling leaves mild redness for two to three days. Deeper chemical peels flake for three to five days. We will tell you the honest answer before you book.

Can I come on my lunch break?

Yes. The LED Light Facial, Dermaplaning and Red Light Body Therapy all run 30 minutes or less with no visible aftereffects.

What if I am pregnant or breastfeeding?

Several treatments remain safe and several do not. Tell us when you book and we will move you to an appropriate option. We do not perform injectable or certain acid treatments during pregnancy.

Do memberships expire if I miss a month?

No. Unused monthly credit rolls forward for three months. After that it lapses, so we send a reminder before you lose anything.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours we charge 50% of the service price, waived once per year for members.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, HSA and FSA cards where the treatment qualifies, and monthly financing through our lending partner. We do not accept personal cheques.

Can I buy a course of treatments upfront?

Yes. Courses of three or six are discounted against single session pricing and never expire. We will only recommend one where the treatment genuinely needs repeating.

Are your injectable services performed by a nurse?

Yes. All injectable treatments are performed by a registered nurse injector working under medical direction. Aestheticians do not administer injectables at either location.
